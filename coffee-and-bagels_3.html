The Space

Part café, part reading nook, part workspace.

Coffee&Bagels sits at street level in Ranjit Avenue, wrapped in greenery and filled with the kind of comfortable seating that makes an hour turn into three. It's the rare Amritsar spot that works equally well for a first date, a solo laptop afternoon, or a Sunday catch-up over cheesecake.

There's a small shelf of books for anyone who wants to read instead of scroll, warm lighting that holds through the evening, and a team that remembers regular orders faster than regulars remember them.
